# Bloom

A flower on your desktop that breathes. You match it. Your breathing slows.

It stays on top of everything. Other apps, full screen, every Space. Always on. You do not open Bloom to meditate. It is already there, in the corner of your eye, while you work.

I wanted a meditation app that did not ask you to sit down and close everything. The idea was simple: if a rhythm sits in the corner of your eye all day, you start breathing with it. It works.

## What it does

- **Always on top.** The flower sits above every window. It follows you across Spaces and stays up in full screen. Quit from the menu bar is how you stop it.
- **Menu bar app.** No dock icon. Settings and Quit live under the little circle in the menu bar.
- **Click through.** Clicks pass through the flower to whatever is underneath, unless you grab it to drag.
- **Drag it.** Put it where your eyes already go.
- **Colors.** Open Settings and pick a seed color, or one of the presets. The petals follow.
- **Pace.** Five breathing rhythms. Same Settings menu.

  - 3 seconds in, 3 out
  - 4 seconds in, 4 out
  - 5 seconds in, 5 out (the default)
  - 6 seconds in, 6 out
  - 4 seconds in, 6 out

- **Local.** No account. No subscription. Nothing leaves your Mac.

## The story

I built this in April 2026 because I wanted something calm that stayed out of the way. Not a session. Not a streak. Just a metronome you can see without looking at it.

Known bug: it struggles on more than one monitor. I have not fixed that yet.

## Status
